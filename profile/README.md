# myDMDi Technology & Design Corp.

**Technology education, mentorship, and augmented civic services for communities across Canada.**

myDMDi is a Canadian technology and design organization. Our mission is to provide
the greatest possible access to technology education and mentorship across
communities throughout Canada — in person and virtual.

🌐 [mydmdi.com](https://mydmdi.com) · ✉️ [info@mydmdi.com](mailto:info@mydmdi.com) · 📍 Regina, Saskatchewan, Canada

---

## Research areas

| Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Augmented civic services** | Community access hubs that extend public services |
| **Solar Autonomous Computing (SAC)** | Solar-powered, low-emission edge computing |
| **AI generative technologies** | Automation, agentic workflows, local-first inference |
| **Quantum security** | Post-quantum cryptography, multi-party key custody |
| **360 / VR immersive storytelling** | Place-based narrative and cultural preservation |
| **IoT & remote sensing** | Environmental monitoring at community scale |
